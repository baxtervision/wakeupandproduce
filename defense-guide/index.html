Basketball Defensive Systems

By Baxter · Mountain View HS Girls Basketball

Explore man, zone, press, and junk defenses through animated alignments, teaching cues, counters, and a roster-based decision guide.

Man Defenses

Man Coverage

Assignment-based defense. Includes man-to-man, pack-line, and switching schemes. Best when you have athletic matchups.

Zone Defenses

Zone Coverage

Area-based shells that protect the paint and force perimeter decisions. 2-3, 3-2, and 1-3-1 schemes.

Press Defenses

Full-Court Press

Tempo-changing pressure applied before the offense sets. 2-2-1, 1-2-2, and diamond press shapes.

Junk Defenses

Junk & Hybrid

Changeup defenses that blend man and zone to neutralize specific offensive threats. Box-and-1, triangle-and-2, matchup zone.

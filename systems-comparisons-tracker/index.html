The Read & React Methodology

Rick Torbett's design gives cognitive independence to the ball handler while structuring precise movements for all other reactors. Movement responds to drives, passes, cuts, and lateral dribbles.

  • 17 to 20 Layers: Build developmental layers progressively.
  • Circle Movement: Fill space automatically relative to penetration.
  • Pass and Cut: Hard basket cuts on 1-spot-away passes.

The Grinnell System Strategy

"The System" uses extreme pacing, transition rules, constant 1-2-2 trapping, and platoon rotations to neutralize athletic disadvantages and create chaos.

  • Relentless Tempo: Goal of a shot attempt every 5-12 seconds.
  • 1-2-2 Trapping Press: Continuous full-court trap pressure.
  • Platoon Subs: Waves of 5 every 45-90 seconds.

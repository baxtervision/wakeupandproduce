The Geometry of Modern Offense

Welcome to the interactive digital playbook. Modern offense is defined by spacing, pace, and sequential decision-making. This guide breaks down 10 effective offensive actions used by elite teams today. Rather than rigid plays, these are dynamic actions designed to force defensive rotations and exploit advantages through read-and-react principles.

How to Use This Playbook

  • Select an action from the sidebar to view its breakdown.
  • Analyze the schematic diagram to understand initial spacing and movement.
  • Study the reads to understand player decision-making.
  • Review examples to see the theory applied in game-like contexts.

Core Principles Assumed

  • Spacing: Minimum 4-out, often 5-out alignments.
  • Advantage Creation: Forcing two defenders onto the ball.
  • 0.5 Second Rule: Once the ball swings, catch-and-shoot, drive, or pass immediately.

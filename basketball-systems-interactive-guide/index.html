System Deep Dive

The Grinnell System

Created by David Arseneault at Grinnell College, this radical offensive philosophy relies on shot volume, three-pointers, and creating chaos.

The Mathematical Goals

The entire system is evaluated against strict per-game metrics.

Total Shots AttemptedGoal: 94+
3-Point AttemptsGoal: 50% of Total Shots
Offensive Rebound %Goal: 33%
Opponent TurnoversGoal: 32

Ideal Shot Distribution

Mid-range shots are discouraged. The offense hunts layups, dunks, and open three-pointers.

1.

First Available Shot

Players take the first open shot available, preferably within the first 12 seconds.

2.

Constant Pressure

The team traps and presses full court, accepting risk to increase tempo and force turnovers.

3.

Hockey Shifts

Five fresh players substitute every 45 to 90 seconds to sustain pressure.
